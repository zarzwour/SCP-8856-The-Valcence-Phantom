Addendum 8856-Ω
Near XK-Class Event: Atmospheric Cascade Test

Incident Designation: 8856-Ω-A
Location: ███████ High Energy Vacuum Laboratory
Clearance: Level 5 (Top Secret)
Date: ██/██/20██


Test Objective

Researchers attempted to observe the behavior of Mode A: Oxidative Strip when applied to atmospheric gases in an oxygen gas rich environment.

Because uncontrolled atmospheric reactions were considered possible, the experiment was conducted inside a reinforced spherical vacuum chamber with the following parameters:

Chamber diameter: ███████ft

Internal pressure: 0.02 atm

Gas composition: 99.9% Nitrogen

Observation window: ███████ layered borosilicate composite

External environment: Vacuum sealed chamber



A small volume of nitrogen gas contained within a glass sphere was inserted into SCP-8856’s tuning chamber.

The device was positioned so that the 30° cone of influence would intersect the center of the vacuum chamber without the span of the cone exceeding the chamber.


Purpose of the test:

Determine whether electron stripping of common atmospheric gasses would remain localized or propagate through adjacent gas.



Timeline
T+00:00

Chamber and corresponding sub-facility evacuated.

Nitrogen test gas released into the center of the chamber.

Device charge measured at ███ Terajoules.



T+00:01

Mode A activation.

Within the cone of influence the nitrogen gas instantly ionizes.

Molecular bonds break apart as electrons are stripped from nitrogen atoms.

The result is an expanding plasma filament approximately 15ft long.

Initial results appear consistent with predictions.



T+00:02

The ionized nitrogen begins pulling electrons from adjacent nitrogen molecules outside the direct cone.

Researchers initially believe this is normal plasma diffusion. However the reaction does not decay. Instead its accelerating.



T+00:03

The ionized region begins spreading radially.

A glowing column of plasma forms along the entire perimeter the firing cone.

The chamber interior fills with intense blue-white light.

Sensors detect extreme electromagnetic levels & Radioactivity.



T+00:04

The reaction begins behaving like a cascading ionization reaction.

Every nitrogen molecule that loses electrons destabilizes neighboring molecules.

The plasma column expands beyond the projected cone boundary.

Chamber pressure begins rising rapidly.



T+00:05

Electromagnetic & Radiation Alarms activate.

The reaction is happening too fast for the researchers to fully understand the magnitude of what is happening.

Simulation systems predict the following if the plasma escapes the chamber:

atmospheric nitrogen would continue the reaction

electron stripping could propagate through surrounding air

resulting plasma chain could spread through the atmosphere

Projected worst-case outcome:

planet-wide atmospheric plasma cascade

Classification: XK-Class End-of-the-World Scenario



T+00:06

The plasma front expands to within 1.2 meters of the vacuum chamber wall.

Temperature inside chamber exceeds ████ K.

The containment window begins softening.

The chamber wall begins developing electrostatic fracture lines.



T+00:07

The chamber is flooded with Argon and Neon Gas.

Due to the full valence shell of the gases it displaces the nitrogen slowing the reaction.

A bright orange-red glow is observed.

T+00:10

Runaway reaction is slowed and becomes less chaotic


T+00:15

All reactions cease. Containment is deemed safe.


Damage:

Vacuum chamber interior:

Severe plasma erosion

Partial vitrification of interior walls

Observation window destroyed

Distance between plasma front and chamber wall at termination measured at 1.2ft


Post-Incident Analysis:

Foundation physicist determined that the experiment nearly created a runaway atmospheric ionization reaction.

If the plasma cascade had breached the vacuum chamber, the reaction could have entered Earth's atmosphere resulting in an XK-Class End of World Scenario.

Computer models indicate the cascade could have propagated globally within minutes.


Estimated consequences:

Ignition of Earths Atmopshere

planet-wide electrical discharges

collapse of biosphere



O5 Council Directive:

All testing involving atmospheric gases with SCP-8856 is permanently banned.

Approved tuning materials are now restricted to:

Tungsten

Copper

Kevlar

Granite

Controlled Metallic Alloys

Testing on nitrogen, oxygen, or carbon compounds requires unanimous O5 authorization.



O5 Commentary

“We almost turned the sky into a plasma reactor.”
