Addendum 8856-01
Discovery Log

Location: Abandoned WWII-era bunker complex, ███████, Germany
Date of Recovery: ███████
Responding Units: Foundation Archaeological Survey Team ███████ & MTF ███████

The bunker found to contain SCP-8856 was initially discovered during a Foundation investigation into undocumented ███████ research facilities that were believed to have collapsed near the end of World War II.

The bunker complex was found to be buried under 100-150 feet of earth and believed to have collapsed near 194█–194█ based on structural dating and corrosion analysis.

Entry was achieved through excavation of a collapsed ventilation shaft.

The first anomaly was discovered immediately upon breaching the inner laboratory corridor.



Initial Observations:

Foundation personnel reported a distinct cone-shaped path of devastation extending roughly 600 feet through multiple reinforced bunker walls.

Within this corridor:

Reinforced concrete walls were cleanly atomized

Steel support beams seemingly decimated and shows evidence of extreme heat

Surfaces showed severe electrostatic pitting

The destruction appeared to follow a distinct path.

Outside this path, the bunker remained almost completely intact.

This geometric pattern was the first indicator of an anomalous device.



Laboratory Chamber

The source of the damage was located inside a sealed research chamber designated Raum-7 in recovered documents.

Inside the chamber, personnel discovered:

A steel research platform

Several destroyed electrical measurement instruments

Extensive radiation shielding structures

A device matching the description of SCP-8856

The device was found mounted on a primitive steel test frame oriented toward a reinforced bunker blast door located at the entrance of the chamber.

The blast door shows evidence of being partially vaporized.

The remainder of the door had undergone extreme Coulombic structural failure, with sections peeled outward as if subjected to immense electrostatic repulsion.



Personnel Remains:

Seven individuals believed to be German scientist were discovered within the chamber.

No conventional human remains were present.

investigators found dark carbonized silhouettes embedded directly into the concrete floor and surrounding walls.

These silhouettes strongly resembled the well-known “atomic shadows” observed at Hiroshima following the detonation of nuclear weapons.

However, these specific shadows varied from the Hiroshima “Atomic Shadows”

The silhouettes were etched several millimeters into the concrete

Surrounding areas were vitrified

Traces of extreme electromagnetic discharge were detected

The positions of the silhouettes suggest the individuals were:

standing near instrumentation

observing the test apparatus

positioned behind shielding barriers

With all evidence it is estimated that the personnel were instantaneously converted to plasma during a high-energy discharge event.



Additional Environmental Evidence

Upon further investigation of the Bunker complex, investigators found further anomalous signs indicating multiple prior test firings:


Observation A (Test Corridor):

A separate hallway showed repeated conical craters in reinforced concrete.

Fragments of steel doors displayed violent electrostatic fracture patterns consistent with Mode A: Oxidative Strip.



Observation B (Containment Pit):

A deep concrete pit contained several heavily damaged test objects:

Armored Steel Plates

Granite blocks

Melted Instrumentation Frames

Each object exhibited identical Coulombic atomization signatures.



Observation C  (Electrical Infrastructure):

The bunker contained an unusually extensive and complex grounding system for the time period.

Large copper rods had been driven over 40 feet into surrounding bedrock, connected to heavy cables.

Several rods had been partially vaporized and melted, indicating extreme electrostatic discharge events.


Recovery:

Upon discovery, SCP-8856 was found in an inactive state with all mechanical dials set to Mode A.

The upper funnel chamber contained traces of Tungsten and Uranium residue, suggesting the device had previously been tuned to multiple materials.

Transport required the construction of a temporary grounding array to prevent uncontrolled electrostatic buildup.

SCP-8856 was transferred to ███████ containment at ███████ on ███████



Researcher Notes:

Dr. ███████ — Initial Analysis

“The Nazis clearly didn’t understand what they built.

They treated it like a directed energy weapon.

But judging by the grounding rods and the damage in this room, they must have triggered a charge cascade they couldn’t control.

Those shadows on the floor?

That wasn’t a weapon test.

That was the moment they realized they had built something far worse.”
